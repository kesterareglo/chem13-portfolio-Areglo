## Source-use note

PubChem is used for compound identity and molecular data; EFSA for caffeine safety context; NIOSH for acetone occupational hazards; and the NIST Chemistry WebBook as a reference resource for physical-property verification. Check the current source pages and relevant safety data sheets before relying on values for laboratory or process design.
